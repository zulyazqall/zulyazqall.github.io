**Mt. Moiwa!**

One of tourist destination near sapporo, and easy access from city to moiwa ropeway by trem, bus or taxi. 

## Traking

I love traking so much because I can release stress and found happiness. This is the way to escape from borring daily live or working live. So this year at 2026, I challanged my self to try winter tracking, but I didn't have any equipment to hike. Because of that, I bought pair of tracking shoes and bag. 
January 4th, I woke up early and went to namboku station to catched first JR train to Otaru, we have planed to hike Mt.Tenguyama, it's located 15 minutes from Otaru station by bus. Unfortunetly, We didn't have permission from Tenguyama official. We changed the plan to Mt. Moiwa. Its took 2 hours until top of Mt.Moiwa, its just fantastic experience.

## What is Miyukidemy

Miyukidemy is an online course platform to especially learn about techology and robotic for kids. In this era, we can't live without gadget and technology thing, but sometimes we lack of knowledge about it. So this website come to you as solution for your kids to enjoy the technology and robotic. We serve fun education and growth mindset.

## Why 

THe purpose is to build integrated school with technology, so making website platform for education become our first step before we make physical school. In the other hand, we start to make robotic product to immerse learning and teaching process.
